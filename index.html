How to play
  • WASDMove. You only move when you press them: the cursor never walks for you.
  • MouseAim. Your serves, spikes, tips and over-balls go where the cursor points (the white ring on the other court), and as setter you set the hitter it points at.
  • Left clickOn the ground: receive. In the air above the net: spike. Hold to wind up, let go to swing. Hold to toss your serve and let go as it drops.
  • ESet. Hold it to charge while you run in: if the meter is full when you touch the ball it is a quick set, low and fast; before that, a high set.
  • Right clickOn the ground: dive for a low or wide ball. In the air: put your block up. A block does not use one of your team's 3 touches.
  • Space (hold)Charge your jump, let go to jump. A full charge jumps higher than a tap. Jump while running and you keep your speed in the air.
  • ShiftStrafe: your Pokémon keeps facing the cursor while you move, and gets up faster after a dive to the side.
  • C / VCamera: follow you or hold still / zoom (close, normal, wide). The mouse wheel zooms too.
  • 1 to 4Quick chat: Mine! (your teammates leave the ball to you), Set me! (your setter looks for you), Nice!, My bad.
  • FArm your Type Spike when the meter is full
  • PPause MSound

The camera sits behind your team, looking at the net. When a ball comes to you, a thin wind-up meter appears beside your Pokémon. Press and hold the left button and it fills upward: meet the ball, or let go just before, with the fill in the gold notch at the top for a PERFECT touch. The small dot beside the meter shows how full it will be when the ball reaches you. Hold too long and the meter turns red for a bad hit. A good wind-up makes your pass land on target and your spikes and serves hit harder, a weak one sprays it. If you do not hold anything you still pass it, just less cleanly. Setting with E uses its own blue meter, which never goes red: it only decides between a high set and a quick one. In 6 v 6, when the pass comes to you as setter, a gold tag shows which hitter the cursor points at. Each side gets 3 touches. Four orange carets on the floor close in on where the ball will land, and when a teammate sets you a green ring shows where to jump. To block, stand at the net, jump just after the other team's hitter jumps and right click. A red ring at the net shows where. Before the draft you choose your position, and you get three Pokémon suited to it to pick from: Setter, Outside hitter, Middle blocker, Opposite or Libero in 6 v 6 (with real rotations and the overlap rule: stand on the green YOUR SPOT ring when the ball is served), or Blocker or Defender in 2 v 2. On a touch screen, use the on-screen buttons; shots then go to the open spot on their own.

Ratings come from each Pokémon's base stats: SPD from Speed, JMP from Speed and Attack (heavy Pokémon jump lower), PWR from Attack and Sp. Atk, DEF from HP and Defense, SKL from Sp. Atk and Sp. Def.
