How to play

  • WASD or arrow keysMove around the court
  • SpaceJump
  • J or clickServe. In the air: spike. On the ground: tip it over.
  • K or ShiftArm your Type Spike when the meter is full
  • PPause MSound

The camera sits behind your team, looking at the net. Touch the ball without pressing anything and you pass it to a teammate automatically. Each side gets 3 touches. An orange ring on the sand shows where the ball will land, and when a teammate sets you a green ring shows where to jump. When you spike, it goes to the open spot on its own; hold left or right to aim across, forward for a short shot or back to hit deep. To block, stand at the net and jump just after the other team's hitter jumps. A red ring at the net shows where, and flashes JUMP when it is time. Before each match you choose your position: Setter, Outside hitter, Middle blocker, Opposite or Libero in 6 v 6 (with real rotations and the overlap rule: stand on the green YOUR SPOT ring when the ball is served), or Blocker or Defender in 2 v 2.

Ratings come from each Pokémon's base stats: SPD from Speed, JMP from Speed and Attack (heavy Pokémon jump lower), PWR from Attack and Sp. Atk, DEF from HP and Defense, SKL from Sp. Atk and Sp. Def.
