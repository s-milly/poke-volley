VolleyDex

Draft a team of Gen 1 Pokémon and spike your way to the win.

How to play

Tutorial

New here? Play short drills with a coach who tells you what to do. Nothing counts and you can stop any time.

Tutorial mode

The basics

  1. Each side gets 3 touches: pass, set, spike.
  2. Orange arrows show where the ball will land. Get there.
  3. Hold left click as the ball comes, let go as it arrives. Let go in the gold notch for a PERFECT hit.
  4. When a teammate sets you, get to the green carets, jump and click to spike.

Controls

  • WASDMove
  • MouseAim your shots and sets
  • Left clickPass, spike or serve: hold, then let go. On a trackpad, tap early instead (tap twice to serve)
  • ESet: tap for a high set, hold for a quick one. Before you serve, switch between a float and a topspin serve
  • SpaceJump: hold to jump higher
  • Right clickDive, or block in the air
  • ShiftStrafe
  • CVCamera and zoom
  • 1-4Quick chat
  • TabHold for the box score
  • P MPause, sound
More tips
  • Holding too long turns the meter red and sprays the ball.
  • Spike with the meter at PERFECT and your Pokémon lets loose its type move, with a ball trail in its type's style. The other team can do it too.
  • To block, stand at the net and jump just after their hitter does, then right click.
  • In 6 v 6, stand on the green YOUR SPOT ring when the ball is served.
  • Ratings come from base stats: SPD Speed, JMP Speed and Attack, PWR Attack and Sp. Atk, DEF HP and Defense, SKL Sp. Atk and Sp. Def.
  • On a touch screen, use the on-screen buttons.
